Why Thalassemia?

Understand the condition

Thalassemia, explained simply.

Clear, general educational information for students, families, teachers and visitors.

01

What is Thalassemia?

Thalassemia is an inherited blood disorder that affects the body's ability to produce healthy hemoglobin.

02

How Does It Affect People?

Different forms vary in severity. Some people may experience significant anemia and require ongoing medical care.

03

Why Are Transfusions Important?

People with severe forms may need regular blood transfusions as part of their medical care.

04

Why Does Blood Donation Matter?

Authorized blood banks and hospitals need reliable supplies of safe blood for patients who require transfusions.

Myth vs fact

Let's challenge misconceptions with reliable information.

Myth

Thalassemia is contagious.

Fact: It is an inherited genetic blood disorder and cannot be spread from person to person.
Myth

Everyone experiences thalassemia in exactly the same way.

Fact: Different types and levels of severity mean experiences can differ.

Information is for general educational purposes and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
